How this was built

  • Data: hourly NASA OMNIWeb values for 2025, 8,435 hours after removing missing rows.
  • Target: aurora-likely hours, defined as Kp ≥ 5 (5.5% of hours).
  • Split: time-aware. The first 80% of hours trained the model, the last 20% (1,687 hours, 96 storm hours) tested it.
  • Model: Random Forest, 300 trees, balanced class weights. Auroral Electrojet (AE) is excluded to avoid leaking the answer.
  • Limits: one year of data and one test window. Kp measures geomagnetic activity, not whether you can see the sky from a given city.
  • Illustrative parts: the aurora oval, city visibility and the sandbox are simplified teaching visuals. Everything in the charts above them is computed from the real test predictions.
